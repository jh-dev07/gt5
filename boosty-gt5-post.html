Attention!
Phone must be on Chinese Android 13 firmware!
After unlocking, all data from the phone will be erased!
Everything you do, you do at your own peril and risk!
Let's get started:
1. Download and install the Deep Test application for GT5:
apk
GT5-DeepTesting.apk6.74 Mb
And download this apk, it will be required later:
apk
DeepTesting_old.apk4.35 Mb
2. Log in to your HeyTap account (Yes, realmi added a new item, this was not necessary before);
3. Go to “Settings” → “About Device” → “Version”, click “Build Number” 7 times to open Developer Mode;
4. Go to “Settings” → “Advanced Settings” → “For Developers”, and turn on the “Factory unlocking” option, aka OEM unlocking;
5. Translate the system language to English and launch the GT5 Deep Test application.
6. In the window that opens, click on Apply button, then check the box and click on the green button again. An inscription and a number will appear in the last window. This number means when you will receive permission to unlock the bootloader. That's it, at this point we press the green button again and wait for the appointed day.
Now delete installed GT5 deeptesting app and install DeepTesting_old.apk
open it and press on Query approval status. You should see a new message above the green button. There will be written Start deep test. Click on the inscription and the phone will reboot into Bootloader
8. Download and install drivers for Fastboot
zip
usb_driver_r13-windows.zip8.28 Mb
9. Download and unpack the Platform-tools folder to your desktop
zip
platform-tools_r34.0.5-windows.zip5.64 Mb
10. Open the Platform-tools folder and launch CMD from it (write cmd in the line where the path to your folder is written)
11. In CMD, enter the command:
fastboot devices
To see if your phone is detected. If so, then we continue, and if you are undecided, then double-check the drivers and update the drivers for your connected device from Device Manager
12. Once the phone is detected, write the following command:
fastboot flashing unlock
Use the volume button to select UNLOCK THE BOOTLOADER and confirm with the power button.
13. That's all, the phone is unlocked. It will reboot and everything will be deleted from your phone.
